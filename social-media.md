Social Media Search Techniques

Search by Username

"username123"

Search for an exact username across indexed websites.

Search Within a Specific Platform

site:reddit.com "username123"

Restrict results to pages indexed from a particular website.

Search for a Public Profile

site:linkedin.com/in "Full Name"

Look for publicly indexed professional profiles matching a name.

Search for a Public Post

site:reddit.com "exact phrase"

Look for indexed posts containing a specific phrase.

Replace example names and usernames with the terms relevant to your research.

Verification and Limitations

* A matching username does not establish that two profiles belong to the same person.
* Display names may be shared by multiple individuals.
* Search engines may show outdated, incomplete, or cached information.
* Private or restricted content should not be accessed through unauthorized means.
* Verify important findings using multiple independent, reliable sources.
* Respect privacy, platform terms, and applicable laws.
