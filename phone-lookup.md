Google libphonenumber

* Repository: https://github.com/google/libphonenumber
* Purpose: Parse, format, and validate phone numbers using international numbering plans.
* Important: Validation checks whether a number matches numbering rules; it does not establish whether the number is active or who owns it.

Carrier and Number Information

Twilio Lookup

* URL: https://www.twilio.com/docs/lookup
* Purpose: Phone number validation and additional lookup information through supported services.
* Requirements: API access and potentially paid usage, depending on the features selected.
* Note: Available carrier and line-type information depends on the country, service, and number.

International Telecommunication Union (ITU)

* URL: https://www.itu.int/
* Purpose: Reference for international telecommunications standards and country calling codes.
* Best for: Understanding international numbering conventions.

Spam and Scam Reports

Truecaller

* URL: https://www.truecaller.com/
* Purpose: Caller identification and community-reported spam information, subject to service availability.
* Limitations: Results may be incomplete, inaccurate, or based on community reports.

FTC — Report Fraud

* URL: https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/
* Purpose: Report suspected scams and fraud in the United States.
* Best for: Reporting scam activity rather than identifying a number’s owner.

FCC — Consumer Complaints

* URL: https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov/
* Purpose: Submit complaints about applicable telephone, robocall, and telecommunications issues in the United States.

Verification and Limitations

* Caller ID can be spoofed, so the displayed number may not identify the actual caller.
* Phone numbers can be reassigned or transferred between carriers.
* Reverse lookup results may be outdated or incorrect.
* Number validation does not confirm the identity of the subscriber.
* Community spam reports are allegations, not definitive proof of fraud.
* Avoid publishing private personal information or using lookup services to harass or target individuals.
* Follow applicable laws, service terms, and privacy requirements.
