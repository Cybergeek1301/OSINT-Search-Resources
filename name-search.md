Real Name OSINT Resources

A curated collection of search engines, public directories, professional networks, and reference resources for researching names using publicly available information.

These resources can help locate public profiles, professional biographies, published references, and other relevant information. A name match alone is not proof of identity.

General Name Search

Google

* URL: https://www.google.com/
* Purpose: Search for names appearing in publicly indexed websites, articles, directories, and profiles.
* Example: "Jane Smith"

Bing

* URL: https://www.bing.com/
* Purpose: Find publicly indexed pages, images, news articles, and professional references.

Brave Search

* URL: https://search.brave.com/
* Purpose: Search for publicly available information using an alternative search index.

Professional Profiles

LinkedIn

* URL: https://www.linkedin.com/
* Purpose: Search public professional profiles, career histories, organizations, and professional publications.

Google Scholar

* URL: https://scholar.google.com/
* Purpose: Find academic publications and author profiles associated with a name.

ORCID

* URL: https://orcid.org/
* Purpose: Search researcher identifiers and scholarly contributions.
* Best for: Academic and scientific identity research.

Public Records

USA.gov

* URL: https://www.usa.gov/
* Purpose: Find official government websites and public-service resources.
* Best for: Locating appropriate government agencies and official record resources.

National Archives

* URL: https://www.archives.gov/
* Purpose: Search historical records and archival collections maintained by the U.S. National Archives.

State and Local Government Websites

* Purpose: Locate official public-record resources, court information, business registrations, and other records where public access is authorized.

Search Techniques

Exact Name Search

"Jane Smith"

Search for an exact name or phrase.

Name and Organization

"Jane Smith" company

Look for public references connecting a name with an organization.

Name and Professional Role

"Jane Smith" engineer

Narrow results using a publicly known profession or area of expertise.

Search Within a Website

site:linkedin.com/in "Jane Smith"

Search for indexed professional profiles on a specific platform.

Search for Published Documents

"Jane Smith" filetype:pdf

Find indexed PDF documents that mention a name.

Replace the example name and terms with the information relevant to your research.

Verification and Limitations

* Many people share the same name.
* Names may change, be abbreviated, or appear in different formats.
* Search results can be outdated or incorrectly attributed.
* Confirm findings using multiple independent sources.
* Prefer official sources when verifying professional credentials or public records.
* Do not infer sensitive personal details from a name match.
* Respect privacy, applicable laws, and website terms of service.

Maintenance

Review links periodically and add reputable resources as they become available.

When suggesting a resource, include its name, official URL, purpose, and any access restrictions or limitations.
